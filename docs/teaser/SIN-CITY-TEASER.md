# X Coin — Fair Launch Teaser

**Style:** Sin City. Black. White. Rain. One color only: the X, dead white, then a single slash of red on the final mark. No borrowed characters. No green. No mining rigs.

**Length:** 48 seconds  
**Launch:** 9:12 PM ET, September 12, 2026  
**Credit:** Nifty Raven (@NFTRVN)  
**Status:** private test until that clock.

---

## VOICE

Low. Dry. Close mic. A man who has already seen the ending and is not impressed.

## PICTURE

Hard cuts. Ink shadows. Wet asphalt. A city drawn in slashes, not photography. Silence between lines is part of the cut.

---

### 0:00 — BLACK

Rain. One drop hits. White.

**VO:** This city runs on names.

A neon sign flickers and dies. Only the rain stays lit.

**VO:** Not hash. Not luck you can buy.

### 0:08 — THE STREET

Boots. A coat. A phone face-down in a puddle, screen black. Someone reaches for it. The hand stops.

**VO:** You don't type a stranger's name and walk away clean.

Close on the phone. A single button burns white: **SIGN IN.**

**VO:** You sign in. Or you don't get in.

### 0:16 — THE ROOM

A wallet, not a terminal. Buttons. Receive. Send. One card with a handle. No command line. No green coin. The window is a comic panel.

**VO:** Every minute, the city picks.

A clock with no numbers. The second hand is a white slash. It lands. Somewhere, one light goes on. Then the street goes dark again.

**VO:** One node. Then two. Then more. No mine. No throne. No first pile.

### 0:28 — THE MARK

Hands. A card passed. An address that starts with X. The transfer is a white line between two silhouettes. It does not ask permission.

**VO:** Fair means the first block pays no one. Fair means the name on the asset is the name you signed in with.

The silhouette turns. Face never shown. Only the X on the lapel, white.

### 0:38 — THE CLOCK

Rain stops for one beat.

Title, broken type, white on black:

**X COIN**  
**XFER**

Under it, smaller:

**09.12.26**  
**9:12 PM**

**VO:** Bring your name. Leave the rest.

### 0:44 — RED

The X tears itself in one red stroke. Holds. Cuts to black.

No URL. No exchange. No "mining begins."

End card, barely there:

**NIFTY RAVEN**  
**@NFTRVN**
